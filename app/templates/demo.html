Room-Temperature Suzuki–Miyaura Cross-Coupling in Aqueous Methanol Using a Recyclable Pd/C Catalyst

Park, S. H.; Müller, K. T.; Vasquez, R. E.; Tanaka, H.

Org. Lett. · 2020 · 10.0000/cr.example Demo paper

View paper ↗

Abstract

A heterogeneous Pd/C catalyst system enables Suzuki–Miyaura couplings in aqueous methanol at room temperature without phosphine ligands. The protocol is operationally simple, applicable to a range of aryl halides and arylboronic acids, and the catalyst can be recovered and reused up to five times without significant loss of activity.

Repro observation

Repeated the coupling of 4-bromoacetophenone with phenylboronic acid (Table 1, entry 4) at 2 mmol scale. Isolated 79% yield after extraction and a short silica column, consistent with the reported 82%. Reaction was clean with no biaryl side products observed.

⚠️ Catalyst recycling: achieved 3 cycles, not 5. Yields dropped 79% → 68% → 51% with simple filtration & washing between cycles.

4 – Reproduced, extension failed

Repro observation

Original protocol reproduced cleanly for aryl bromides (Table 1, entries 1–6). Yields within ±5% of reported values. Reaction setup and workup are straightforward.

Scope extension

Attempted extension to an aryl chloride substrate. Only 10% conversion after 24 h under standard conditions; aryl chlorides are incompatible with Pd/C at room temperature. Switching to Pd(PPh3)4 at 60 °C in dioxane gave 71% yield. The paper’s scope claims are accurate for the substrate classes described.
